Product Specification: Email Router

Overview:
The Email Router is an intelligent email management system that automatically
classifies, prioritizes, and routes incoming emails to the appropriate team,
department, or individual within an organization.

Problem Statement:
Organizations receive large volumes of email to shared inboxes (support@,
info@, sales@). Manually triaging these emails is slow, error-prone, and leads
to missed messages, slow response times, and inconsistent handling.

Target Users:
- Customer support teams handling shared inboxes
- Operations managers overseeing email workflows
- Sales teams receiving inbound leads
- IT administrators configuring routing rules

Core Capabilities:
1. Email Classification: Automatically categorize incoming emails by topic,
   intent, and urgency using natural language understanding.
2. Intelligent Routing: Route each email to the correct team, queue, or
   individual based on classification results and configurable rules.
3. Priority Detection: Identify urgent or high-value emails (e.g., outage
   reports, VIP customers) and escalate them.
4. Auto-Response: Send acknowledgment emails with expected response times.
5. Routing Rules Management: Admin interface for creating, testing, and
   managing routing rules without code.
6. Analytics Dashboard: Track email volumes, routing accuracy, response
   times, and team workloads.
7. Integration: Connect with common email providers (Gmail, Outlook) and
   ticketing systems (Zendesk, Jira Service Management).

Non-Functional Requirements:
- Process emails within 30 seconds of receipt
- 99.9% uptime
- SOC 2 compliant handling of email content
- Support organizations with up to 100,000 emails per day
